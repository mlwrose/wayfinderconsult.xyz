POP DROP

A novelty photo booth for events in the Netherlands. Guests step in, the booth remixes them into something they weren’t expecting, and a print drops out seconds later. No operator in the room.

Built end to end as a Wayfinder venture: the name and brand, the cabinet, the generative pipeline that runs it, and the booking side behind it. Launching November 2026.

Wayfinder project

NUFF SPACE Storage

Concept, jingle, direction, AI production and edit

A fake 1998 local TV ad for a storage company that found infinite space, made solo in nine days for ElevenLabs’ World’s Catchiest Ad competition. The dancehall jingle came first and became the shot list: every scene was generated to the timing of the track, then graded to one look. 43 seconds, one salesman, no exits.
